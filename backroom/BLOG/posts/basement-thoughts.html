NOTHING BETTER THAN FEELING STRANGE IN A BASEMENT
I lied. Sort of. There is a lot to it I think. For the past month, every night, I go down to my own little slice of heaven. I actually based the basement on this. That, and I fucking LOVED the backroom's movie. Maybe I loved it because this basement looks like that. Same nauseating yellow walls, same warm-but-not-warm lighting, and most of all, the same out of time feeling.

Part of that may just be timing. It definitely looks strange, but I think more than anything it's the time of my life. Everything is in a sort of flux right now. It's on the upswing, but I'm just sitting, ready to go in almost all aspects of my life. But I'm stuck. I can't move forward. I've been sent back in time, and I sit in that basement. Being back in this house, seeing my parents again, seeing my highschool friends again. It's weird. I don't remember basically anything from the ages of 12 to 19, and by then, I became a new man. My parents have no idea who I am, and I can't tell them. All my highschool friends talk about those times, and it feels so strange. I don't remember the names they bring up, the things that happened, so I just sit there in silence. It's almost alienating. I was there for these memories, at least some version of me. But when they bring the events up, it feels as though I wasn't there. It feels like I'm being excluded, but I was there.

It hit a peak recently. Or a trench. Got into an argument about the effectiveness of AI with my dad. I had assumed he changed, that he wasn't the man I remembered from before I forgot everything. But deep down that same man is still there. The same anger, the same ego. "The engineer mindset". Why worry about emotional regulation, thats a waste. Clearly it was more important to study and work and there just wasn't enough time to worry about silly things like the ethics of hitting your child. When he got loud, when he screamed, when he raised his hands, not even conciously but out of instinct, for a moment I was that child again. For a moment, I stopped being Jai, and went back to being Kush.

The worst part wasn't the fear he'd do it. I can take people yelling at me. The worst part is that for the first time in my life, I didn't shut down. I didn't just turn into that kid fully. It wasn't flight this time, it was pure fight. I almost wanted him to hit me. I wanted him to dare to try it. His anger and his ego, all of that went into me, and it was the most disgusting feeling. He went upstairs shortly after, and I had a panic attack. It wasn't one of the worst ones I've had, and shoutout to my ex, Shreya really helped me get through it.

It almost feels unfair for me to talk about him like this. He wasn't always this man. He was a scared little boy once too, with an even worse father, and an even rougher life. I could never do what he did, and I wouldn't be where I am without the path he layed. He really tried to be a good dad, and it's not that he wasn't loving. He genuinely did the best he could. I see the places where my grandfather failed him, and I see him compensating in so many places. But he couldn't get everything. Nobody could. But that is something I can never let happen to my kids. This cycle has to end here.

A lot has happened this summer, and it's only July. Hasn't all been bad, and what has been has also been informative. Even just the moments of lying in that basement, staring at the spider on the ceiling, thinking about how strange it is to be anything at all, its fun. Maybe. Maybe not. But I do think it's important. Informative. I'm so glad to be anything at all, always. Even in the moments where my mind feels like static and I feel outside of time.

Peace and love for all mankind.
